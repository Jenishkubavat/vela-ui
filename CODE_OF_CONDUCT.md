# Code of Conduct

Vela UI is built in the open, and everyone who takes part (in issues, pull
requests, discussions, or anywhere else the project is talked about) is
expected to make it a good place to work.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to Blazor,
  to open source, or to English.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- Critique code and ideas, not people. Explain what you'd change and why.
- Accept a decision you disagree with once it has been made and explained.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's identity, background, or
  ability.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior bothers you, contact the maintainer privately through
[@Jenishkubavat](https://github.com/Jenishkubavat) on GitHub. Reports are kept
confidential. You can also use GitHub's own "Report content" option on any
comment.

## What happens next

The maintainer reviews every report and may ask the person to change their
behavior, remove comments, or block them from the project, depending on how
serious it is and whether it has happened before.
